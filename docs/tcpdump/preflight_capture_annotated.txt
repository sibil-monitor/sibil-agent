during `sibil preflight --connect SIB-CONNECT-9F2K-3LMQ-7XPZ`
=================================================================

This is a worked example with commentary, not a raw pcap dump. Reproduce it
yourself with the commands in README.md — that capture is the actual proof,
this file just tells you what to expect from it.

                — Application data. This is the single HTTPS POST shown

14:02:01.310  IP 188.x.x.x.443 > <your-vps>: Flags [P.], length 31
                — Response: {"status":"ok","compatible":true}

14:02:01.340  IP <your-vps> > 188.x.x.x.443: Flags [F.]
                — Connection closed by the CLI. No keep-alive, no second

What you will NOT see anywhere in this capture:
  - a second destination IP/host
  - a connection that outlives the `sibil preflight` process
  - any DNS lookup other than monitor.cordee.ovh
  - any UDP traffic

If your own capture shows anything beyond one TCP stream to
monitor.cordee.ovh that opens and closes around the single request/response
pair above, that's a bug — open an issue with your (redacted) pcap.
